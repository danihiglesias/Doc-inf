# 2026-27 academic calendar (first-year students)

These are the published dates for those of you who enrolled for the first time in the 2026-27 academic year. They show what has to be done, when and by whom; note that some steps do not depend on you, but they do require you to have submitted your part beforehand.

| Step | Date | Who does it |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Submit the Research and Training Plan (Plan de Investigación y Formación)** | Before the end of March 2027 (we will review it together and I will send you some examples) | The student submits it, endorsed by their supervisors, for approval by the Academic Committee (Comisión Académica) |
| **Upload the initial plan to RAPI** | After the Committee has approved it | The Doctoral School (Escuela de Doctorado), not the student |
| **Upload activities and supporting documents to RAPI** | Throughout the year; ideally complete before 10 June 2027 | The student records them and sets them to “En revisión” (under review) for validation |
| **Add the tutor's/supervisor's annual report** | Before 10 June 2027 | Tutor/supervisor |
| **Submit the annual assessment record** | 26 June 2027 | Academic Committee |

```{admonition} About RAPI
:class: tip

**RAPI (Registro de Actividades y Plan de Investigación — Activities Record and Research Plan)** is the University of Salamanca platform where each doctoral student's work is recorded and monitored. In it, students add the training and research activities they have completed (such as courses, seminars, conferences, publications and research stays) together with their supporting documents, so that their tutor or supervisor can review and validate them. It also brings together the research plan, its progress, the monitoring reports and the Academic Committee's annual assessments, which determine whether the student continues on the programme. Once approved, the initial plan is uploaded by the Doctoral School.

<https://doctorado.usal.es/es/registro-actividades>
```
