# Studium, RAPI and submissions overview

The programme has a **Studium course** in which the coordinator, **Rodrigo Santamaría**, posts news: announcements of seminars and courses, thesis submissions and defences, job offers and any communication that needs to reach you quickly. Check it regularly and turn on notifications. The same course contains a summary of the [programme's training activities](https://studium26.usal.es/course/section.php?id=27491), which are set out in detail in the Doctoral School's official programme sheet.

All activities must be uploaded to the **RAPI** platform with their supporting document and accepted by your tutor or thesis supervisor. The table below summarises, year by year, which activities must be completed and submitted, when, where, who signs or accepts them and who uploads them to RAPI.

| Year | Document | Date | Where to submit | Signed / accepted by | Uploaded to RAPI by |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1** | **Research Plan**, plus the addition of a co-supervisor if needed (approved in April by the Academic Committee, to which the School forwards it) | Before the end of March 2027 | By email to n.doctorado@usal.es | Thesis supervisor | School |
| **From year 2 (every year)** | **Progress report** on the development of the doctoral thesis | From now until early June 2027 | RAPI (Activities) | Thesis tutor (accept in RAPI) | Student |
| | **Tutor's report** | June / September | RAPI (Assessments) | Tutor | Tutor |
| | **Meetings with the tutor**: **at least 12 h over the course of the thesis** (we will hold as many meetings as you need, but at least these must be documented). State attendees, date and duration in hours | June (deadline) | RAPI (Activities) | Accepted in RAPI by the tutor | Student |
| **First 2 years (3 if part-time)** | **Training courses**: supporting documents showing the number of hours; **at least 15 h in total**. Courses offered by the Doctoral School's Transversal Training Programme (Programa de Formación Transversal) count, as does any course at a university or research centre that interests you and is approved by your supervisor | At any time; deadline: June of year 2 (year 3 if part-time) | RAPI (Activities) | Lecturer or body delivering the course; accepted in RAPI by the tutor | Student |

| | **Three national or international conferences** with a paper presented; one of them **must be the doctoral conference (jornadas de doctorado)** (April-May) | | RAPI (Activities) | Certificate from the organisers confirming the paper was presented (attendance alone does not count); accepted by the tutor | Student |

```{admonition} Important
:class: warning

If, at the June assessment, the required activities are not recorded, are not properly supported or there is a negative report from the tutor, the Academic Committee will grade the student as **FAIL (NO APTO)**. As a second sitting, the activities and the tutor's report are reviewed again in September; if they are still missing or the report is again negative, the grade will be FAIL (NO APTO), meaning the student has not successfully completed the doctoral programme.
```
