# Academic record and annual monitoring

Every academic year, check that your academic record (expediente) is up to date. It must show:

- Current enrolment and continuation status, with no permanent withdrawal.
- Formally appointed tutor and thesis supervisors, with the supervision agreement added to the record.
- Research and Training Plan approved before the end of the first year, endorsed by everyone responsible for supervision and tutoring.
- Activities Record (Documento de Actividades del Doctorando) kept up to date in RAPI, with supporting documents and validation by the tutor/supervisor.
- Annual monitoring reports and favourable assessments by the Academic Committee (Comisión Académica), with no assessment or correction pending.

In RAPI, the initial plan is approved by the Committee and uploaded by the School; from the second year onwards, it is you who add the plan's progress and your activities.
