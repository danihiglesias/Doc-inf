# Time limits and thesis submission deadline

| First enrolment | Standard duration | Extension |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Before 2023-24** | 3 years full-time / 5 part-time | Previous rules: 1+1 years full-time; 2+1 part-time, subject to authorisation |
| **From 2023-24 (including the 2026-27 cohort)** | 4 years full-time / 7 part-time | Up to 1 year, on request and subject to authorisation |

```{admonition} Calendar alert
:class: warning

The current regulations (art. 8.4) require you to apply for thesis submission (depósito) **before 1 June of your final year of enrolment**. Do not confuse this deadline with the date of the defence itself: submission starts a procedure that takes several months. Students from earlier cohorts should check the first transitional provision and confirm with the School how it applies to their record.
```
