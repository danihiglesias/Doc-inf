# Training activities: minimum requirements and evidence

The programme sets minimum training activity requirements, which are recorded in the Activities Record (Documento de Actividades del Doctorando) in RAPI and validated by the tutor or supervisor. All of these activities can be completed online: none of them requires in-person attendance. They are as follows:

| Activity | Minimum required | Evidence to provide |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Seminars** | 6 hours in total | Attendance and the assessment or reports provided for |
| **Research monitoring** | 12 hours; at least one meeting per semester (a minimum of 6 according to the programme sheet); present progress at least twice from the second year onwards | Record validated by the tutor/supervisor |
| **Methodological, specialised or practical courses** | 15 hours; within the first two years if full-time or three if part-time | Certificates |
| **Conferences or scientific meetings** | 3, with a presentation at each (expected equivalence: 60 hours) | Certificates of attendance and of presentation; programme meetings may count |
| **Scientific publication** | At least one article in English accepted during the thesis in a JCR journal | Manuscript and acceptance letter, or published article |
| **Research stay at another centre** | A 150-hour activity at a Spanish or foreign centre. We will look for a centre that gives you flexibility to complete this requirement | Report and certificate from the person in charge at the host centre |

```{admonition} The JCR publication and the research stay are programme requirements, not optional
:class: important

Having submitted the article is not enough: the programme sheet requires it to be accepted. Research stays need a certificate and validation in RAPI; plan them well in advance with me. The general research stay can be in Spain; the three months abroad apply only to those who wish to apply for the International Doctorate mention.
```
