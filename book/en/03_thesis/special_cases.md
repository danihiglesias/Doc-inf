# Special cases only

## Thesis by compendium of publications

This requires the format to be authorised in the plan or in its annual review; at least three articles or chapters published or accepted after starting the doctorate with the required indexing (exceptionally two, on grounds of documented relevance); being first author on at least two; all contributions being related to the thesis (abstracts, proceedings and reviews do not count); authorisations from the co-authors and a waiver from co-authors without a doctorate to use those works in another thesis; and a dissertation with an integrating introduction, hypotheses and objectives, conclusions, the full publications, acceptance letters and DOIs. This minimum is separate from the JCR article required of all students on the programme.

## International Doctorate mention

This requires stays outside Spain totalling at least three months (if there are several, one must be at least one month long), with endorsement, prior authorisation, certificates and registration in the Activities Record; two reviewers from foreign institutions other than those who hosted you; at least one member of the examination panel from a foreign institution other than the person in charge of the stay; the application for the mention together with the thesis submission; and writing and defending at least the summary and conclusions in the relevant scientific language. The 2024 procedure also excludes certain connections with the host centre (employment ties held by you or your supervisors, and the centre where you obtained your entry qualification), so check it before organising your mobility.
