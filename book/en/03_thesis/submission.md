# What you will need for thesis submission

When the time comes, we will prepare the thesis submission (depósito) file together. It is worth knowing now what it includes:

- The finished thesis, in the format accepted by the Committee and approved by the supervisors, as **a single PDF with all its appendices**.
- If it is written in another language: a second document in Spanish with the title, table of contents, introduction, substantial summary, conclusions and references, endorsed by the supervisors.
- An explicit statement on the use of AI in the thesis, describing its scope and its ethical and methodological limits (also when it has not been used).
- Two reports from expert doctors external to USAL, requested by the supervisors before submission, and your response to their comments with the relevant corrections.
- A report from the supervisors on whether the proposed improvements have been incorporated, and a plagiarism report generated with the institutional tool.
- The submission application on the official form (filed by the supervisors with the Committee) and the electronic archive/repository form; if full publication is not authorised, a justification and an additional PDF with the publishable part.
- A proposal of five examination panel members with their acceptances, meeting the requirements for research experience, an external majority and gender balance.
- Financial documentation for travel and accommodation, budget and co-funding annex, plus the additional documentation for a compendium, international mention or joint supervision (cotutela) where applicable.

External reviewers and panel members must comply with the rules on incompatibility: co-authors, significant collaborators, those in charge of research stays and people with the family or marital ties specified in the regulations are excluded. Supervisors, co-supervisors and tutors cannot sit on the panel either.

## Procedure timescales

The regulations provide for 20 days for the external reports, 15 to respond and correct, 5 to check requirements, up to 30 to assess the thesis after the submission is accepted and 10 for public display, plus the authorisation and the formal notice of the defence.

```{admonition} Do not plan on the assumption that a month will be enough
:class: warning

Bear in mind, too, that the thesis cannot be modified after the submission has been applied for.
```

After submission, the remaining steps are approval to continue the procedure, public display, appointment of the three-member examination panel, payment of the defence fees, formal notice of the defence at least ten days in advance and the public defence.
