# Applicable regulations and contacts

The programme sheet still links to the 2015 regulations, but the current USAL regulations are those approved on 18 July 2025, published in the BOCYL on 15 December 2025 and in force from the following day. The 2015 document is for historical reference only, not the current submission checklist. To finalise a specific record, you must confirm with the School anything that depends on it: individual deadline, transitional application of the 1 June deadline, recognition of activities and possible equivalences for the JCR article. There is additional internal programme information in the Studium course (see [Studium, RAPI and submissions overview](../01_year/studium_rapi.md)).
