# About

This guide brings together the requirements and deadlines that doctoral students on the Doctoral Programme in Computer Engineering (Programa de Doctorado en Ingeniería Informática) at the University of Salamanca need to know during the 2026-27 academic year: calendar, use of Studium and RAPI, training activities, annual monitoring, thesis submission and thesis types. It is support material prepared by the thesis supervisor for his doctoral students and does not replace the official regulations or the instructions of the Doctoral School (Escuela de Doctorado).

## How this book is made

This site is written in Markdown files and converted to HTML and PDF using tools from the [TeachBooks](https://teachbooks.io/), [Jupyter Book](https://jupyterbook.org/) and [MyST Markdown](https://mystmd.org/) ecosystem, based on the [TeachBook USAL template](https://github.com/elloza/teachbook_usal_template) by Álvaro Lozano Murciego and André Filipe Sales Mendes.
