# Doctoral student guide: requirements and deadlines

**Doctoral Programme in Computer Engineering (Programa de Doctorado en Ingeniería Informática) · University of Salamanca · 2026-27 academic year**

This document summarises what you need to bear in mind during your doctorate: the deadlines for the 2026-27 academic year, how to use Studium and RAPI, the compulsory training activities, the annual monitoring and the requirements for reaching thesis submission (depósito) and defence. Read it carefully, keep it to hand and come back to it when planning each academic year.

```{admonition} A general checklist
:class: note

This is a general checklist: checking your own particular case depends on the date of your first enrolment, whether you are full-time or part-time, your annual assessments and the activities already validated, so if in doubt, ask me or the Doctoral School (Escuela de Doctorado).
```
